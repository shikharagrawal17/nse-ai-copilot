# Brag Plan: DalalDesk

## What is this app?
DalalDesk is an institutional-grade Indian equity research terminal connected directly to official NSE Capital Market Live Feeds and Bhavcopy databases via the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

## The angle
DalalDesk delivers zero-hype, real-time quantitative intelligence for Dalal Street. While retail traders drown in noisy social tips and generic AI summaries, DalalDesk connects directly to official exchange market pipelines, computing Camarilla pivots, ATR volatility bands, and institutional trade plans in milliseconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast trading floor display: "NSE Capital Market Live Feeds: Connected." The benchmark ticker tape surges across the screen with NIFTY 50 and NIFTY 500 flashing live green prints.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Live Indices Tape: Real-time NSE broad-market pulse streaming NIFTY 50 (+1.21%) and NIFTY 500 (+1.16%).
- The Quantitative Trade Plan: Autonomous algorithmic breakdown for institutional swing setups featuring CMP, Camarilla pivots, 2.35x ATR target expansions, and statistical conviction metrics.
- The 36 NSE MCP Tools Explorer: Direct streaming execution of live quotes, bhavcopy breadth, and volume surges with sub-20ms latency counters.

## Outro / punchline
"Institutional execution. Zero AI hype." Crisp DalalDesk logo lockup with official NSE MCP badge and GitHub release tag.

## User flow worth showing
- Entry: Ticker tape streams live NSE benchmark market breadth across 3,700 listed equities.
- Key Action: User dispatches institutional query; DalalDesk executes algorithmic Camarilla pivot calculations and ATR risk regimes.
- Result: Quantitative trading plan cards render with entry shelves, conservative targets, and breakout expansions.
